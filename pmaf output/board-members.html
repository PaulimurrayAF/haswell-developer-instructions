Board of Directors

Building the Future with Vision and Integrity

Meet the eight changemakers shaping our strategy, stewarding our values, and guiding the mission of the Pauli Murray Awards Foundation.

Cory L. Scott (He/Him)

Chair, Board of Directers Founding Executive Director

Cory L. Scott is an award-winning author, activist, strategist, pastor, and Black sex educator whose work exists at the intersection of sacred purpose, radical possibility, and transformative justice. Born in Chicago, raised in Washington, DC, and now based in New York City's Bronx borough, Cory creates transformative spaces where Black queer people can embrace their wholeness, joy is viewed as sacred resistance, and truth-telling is an act of spiritual devotion. A proud alumnus of Howard University, Cory studied History and Political Science-foundations that deeply inform his approach to activism, political discourse, and storytelling. His multifaceted journey has included roles as a grief and bereavement care practitioner, Lead Pastor of Transformers Church Chicago, sex educator, nonprofit leader, and theologian, allowing him to connect deeply with diverse audiences through authenticity, compassion, and critical insight. From 2016 until its conclusion in 2022, Cory was a regular cast member, contributor, and producer on The Grapevine TV, a groundbreaking YouTube panel series and digital sensation that sparked crucial conversations on race, gender, identity, and politics, shaping discourse across Black digital communities worldwide. Cory is the author of `The Counterculture Devotional` (2019), a groundbreaking work inviting readers into a faith rooted in radical honesty and liberation, setting the groundwork for his ongoing mission to empower queer Christians. He is currently working on his second book, `Forgotten We Once Flew: Reconciling Queer Identity & Christianity,` exploring profound intersections of faith, sexuality, and spiritual resilience through lived experiences of religious trauma and conversion therapy survivors. As the Founder and Chair of the Board of Directors for the Pauli Murray Awards Foundation, Cory leads an innovative nonprofit dedicated to honoring and elevating Black LGBTQ+ excellence through national awards, mentorship programs, and advocacy initiatives. His leadership is marked by intentionality, precision, and an unwavering commitment to legacy as a spiritual. On a personal note, Cory shares his life with his fiancé, Jordan, their two adult sons, Catrell and Zale, and Royale, a beloved Frenchton inherited from his best friend, Tavares, after his passing. Whether speaking at conferences, writing, or organizing community-building events, Cory moves with clarity-guided by love, grounded in faith, and always prepared for the extraordinary.
