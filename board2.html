BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Leading with Vision and Commitment

The Pauli Murray Awards Foundation is governed by a national Board of Directors committed to the vision, values, and stewardship of this work. Each member brings lived experience and professional expertise in service of our mission.

Meet the visionaries guiding the Foundation’s direction and integrity. Our Board of Directors holds fiduciary responsibility for the Foundation and plays a vital role in shaping our long-term strategy, financial stewardship, and institutional accountability.

Officers: Cory L. Scott — Chair  •  Nyron Rouse, LL.M, MPA — Vice Chair  •  Karyden Roberts — Secretary  •  Darnell Riley, M.Ed, Ed.S — Interim Treasurer

Leadership Opportunities

Help Shape the Future of Black LGBTQ+ Leadership

Board of Directors
We are seeking passionate, values-aligned individuals to help us steward and grow this movement.

Ideal Candidates

  • Reflect the diversity of Black LGBTQ+ communities across gender, geography, and life experience
  • Bring expertise in nonprofit governance, fundraising, law, finance, media, education, policy, or philanthropy
  • Demonstrate a commitment to racial justice, LGBTQ+ equity, and collective liberation

Board Responsibilities Include

  • Quarterly board meetings (virtual and/or in person)
  • Participation on at least one committee
  • Support for fundraising efforts
  • Annual personal contribution at a meaningful level
  • Three-year term, renewable once

Join an Advisory Committee

Help shape the future of Black LGBTQ+ leadership by lending your expertise as a PMAF Advisor. Advisory Committees bring together practitioners, scholars, artists, organizers, and community leaders to inform strategy, strengthen programs, and expand our impact.

Ideal Advisors

  • Diverse across gender, geography, and life experience
  • Expertise in governance, fundraising, law, finance, media, education, policy, philanthropy, or health
  • Sustained commitment to racial justice, LGBTQ+ equity, and collective liberation

What Participation Looks Like

  • Quarterly advisory meetings (virtual and/or in person)
  • Participation in at least one working group aligned to your skillset
  • Ambassadorial support for outreach, partnerships, and visibility
  • Term: one year, renewable based on mutual interest
