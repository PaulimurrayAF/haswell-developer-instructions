Advisors to the Board

Our Advisors bring depth, wisdom, and creative perspective to the Pauli Murray Awards Foundation. Each comes from a diverse background—spanning the arts, academia, activism, health, and culture—and together they enrich the Foundation with counsel, guidance, and a commitment to equity. Advisors are not voting members of the Board of Directors but serve as thought partners, amplifiers, and advocates who help shape the Foundation’s direction and impact.

Joseph Harrington

Advisor

Seasoned nonprofit leader and strategist with deep experience in governance, cultural programming, and organizational development. Joseph centers historically marginalized voices—particularly within Black and LGBTQ+ communities—bringing collaborative vision and operational strength to guide mission-driven growth and impact.

Jonathan McCrory

Advisor

Obie Award–winning artist, director, and cultural curator whose career bridges art and activism. As a longtime leader in Black theater, Jonathan elevates visionary storytelling and cultivates spaces where creativity drives liberation, community-building, and cultural transformation.

Zimife Umeh, PhD

Advisor

Sociologist and researcher examining the intersections of race, gender, inequality, and justice. Dr. Umeh’s work follows the reentry journeys of formerly incarcerated women, pairing rigorous, mixed-methods research with a justice-oriented lens that connects academic insight to real-world equity solutions.

Asia Hodges, MS

Advisor

Clinical Research Associate, medical writer, and regulatory consultant with an MS in Microbiology (University of Florida). Asia brings site-level clinical trial management, four years in regulatory compliance, and a growing focus on health informatics and precision medicine—translating complex data through clear visualization and equity-minded insights.

Join an Advisory Committee

Help shape the future of Black LGBTQ+ leadership by lending your expertise as a PMAF Advisor. Advisory Committees bring together practitioners, scholars, artists, organizers, and community leaders to inform strategy, strengthen programs, and expand our impact.

Ideal Advisors

  • Reflect the diversity of Black LGBTQ+ communities across gender, geography, and life experience
  • Bring expertise in governance, fundraising, law, finance, media, education, policy, philanthropy, or health
  • Demonstrate a sustained commitment to racial justice, LGBTQ+ equity, and collective liberation

What Participation Looks Like

  • Quarterly advisory meetings (virtual and/or in person)
  • Participation in at least one working group aligned to your skillset
  • Ambassadorial support for outreach, partnerships, and visibility
  • Term: one year, renewable based on mutual interest
