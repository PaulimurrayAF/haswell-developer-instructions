AARON MARCELLUS

MUSICAL & ARTISTIC DIRECTOR; MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS
Home / About Us / AARON MARCELLUS

About Aaron

Aaron Marcellus is a multi-hyphenate artist — singer, vocal coach, writer, musician, dancer, and actor — who has performed to audiences worldwide for more than twenty years. Born in Atlanta and rooted in Gospel, he has recorded with Warner Brothers’ Word Records and rose to national prominence as a Top 24 finalist on American Idol (2011). His career spans stage and screen, including a ChapStick commercial, an NBC pilot (Next Caller), and a current role in New York City’s off-Broadway hit STOMP.

Aaron has toured globally — performing across England, Italy, Holland, Germany, France, Korea, Kuwait, and Japan — and is a fixture of New York’s live-music scene, hosting weekly sets at Duane Park while directing showcases, tap dancing, coaching vocalists, and performing throughout the city. He is also the Founder & CEO of Surrender To Love LLC, supporting music and arts programs and providing meals to communities in need.
